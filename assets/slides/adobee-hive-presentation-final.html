The problem

Your next customer may never visit your website.

How does a business stay found, trusted and bookable when an AI agent arrives first?

Can it find you?

Your offer is scattered across pages and platforms.

Can it trust the facts?

Can it complete the task?

Forms and booking steps still expect a human to click.

The architecture

Inside the Adobee Hive.

Local context becomes signed Cards. Agents discover and act through MCP. People stay in control.

The owner reviews a Card draft. The hub publishes a signed JSON version.

Prototype: hub-held signing keys; folder links stay on one machine; federation demo uses three local hubs.
